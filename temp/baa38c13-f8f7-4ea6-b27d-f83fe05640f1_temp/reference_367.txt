Year: 2014 Title: Characterisation of an OCS-dependent severe asthma population treated with mepolizumab Journal: Thorax Volume: 69 Issue: 12 Pages: 1141-2 Epub Date: 20140516 Date: Dec Short Title: Characterisation of an OCS-dependent severe asthma population treated with mepolizumab ISSN: 0040-6376 DOI: 10.1136/thoraxjnl-2014-205581 Accession Number: 24834924 Keywords: Administration, Oral Adrenal Cortex Hormones/*therapeutic use Adult Anti-Asthmatic Agents/*therapeutic use Antibodies, Monoclonal, Humanized/*therapeutic use Asthma/*drug therapy Drug Therapy, Combination Female Humans Male Middle Aged Asthma Eosinophil Biology Abstract: A subpopulation of patients with asthma treated with maximal inhaled treatments is unable to maintain asthma control and requires additional therapy with oral corticosteroids (OCS); a subset of this population continues to have frequent exacerbations. Alternate treatment options are needed as daily use of OCS is associated with significant systemic adverse effects that affect many body systems and have a direct association with the dose and duration of OCS use. We compared the population demographics, medical conditions and efficacy responses of the OCS-dependent group from the DREAM study of mepolizumab with the group not managed with daily OCS.
