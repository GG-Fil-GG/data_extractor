Year: 2016 Title: Reductions in eosinophil biomarkers by benralizumab in patients with asthma Journal: Respir Med Volume: 111 Pages: 21-9 Epub Date: 20160108 Date: Feb Short Title: Reductions in eosinophil biomarkers by benralizumab in patients with asthma ISSN: 0954-6111 DOI: 10.1016/j.rmed.2016.01.003 Accession Number: 26775606 Keywords: Adult Aged Antibodies, Monoclonal, Humanized/*therapeutic use Antibody-Dependent Cell Cytotoxicity Asthma/*blood/*drug therapy/immunology Biomarkers/blood Double-Blind Method Eosinophil Cationic Protein/*blood Eosinophil-Derived Neurotoxin/*blood Eosinophils/*drug effects/immunology/pathology Female Humans Interleukin-5/blood/immunology Male Middle Aged Young Adult Asthma Benralizumab Biomarker Eosinophil Il-5 Abstract: BACKGROUND: Eosinophilic inflammation is frequently associated with increased asthma severity. Benralizumab is a humanized, afucosylated, anti-interleukin-5R  monoclonal antibody that selectively depletes eosinophils and basophils through enhanced antibody-dependent cell-mediated cytotoxicity. OBJECTIVE: To study effects of benralizumab on eosinophil counts and activity following administration to asthma patients. METHODS: Sera were collected from asthma patients enrolled in two clinical studies. Placebo or benralizumab was subcutaneously administered to patients in Phase I (100 or 200 mg, multiple doses; N = 14; NCT00659659) and Phase IIa (25, 100, or 200 mg every 4 weeks; N = 24; NCT00783289) studies. Sera were also collected from healthy volunteers (N = 20) for comparison. Blood eosinophils, IL-5, eosinophil-derived neurotoxin (EDN), eosinophil cationic protein (ECP), eotaxin/chemokine (C-C motif) 11 (CCL11), eotaxin-2/CCL24, tumor necrosis factor (TNF), and interferon-  (IFN- ) were measured at baseline and post-treatment. RESULTS: Increased EDN concentrations were observed in sera of patients from both studies relative to healthy volunteers (p < 0.05). At baseline, sera EDN concentrations correlated with blood eosinophil counts (rs = 0.5; p < 0.05). Benralizumab reduced blood eosinophil numbers and sera EDN and ECP relative to baseline (p < 0.05). No changes in TNF or IFN-  were observed, while serum IL-5, eotaxin/CCL11, and eotaxin-2/CCL24 increased after benralizumab administration vs. placebo (p < 0.05). CONCLUSIONS: In two independent studies, serum IL-5, EDN, and ECP were modulated following benralizumab. Eosinophil depletion after benralizumab also resulted in significant reductions in EDN and ECP concentrations, suggesting that cytotoxic granule proteins were not released after eosinophil reduction.
